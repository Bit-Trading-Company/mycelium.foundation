Questions

Frequently asked.

Short answers to the questions we hear most. If yours isn’t here, write to us.

The network

About the network.

Is the Mycelium Network live?

No. The network is in its research phase and the testnet is in preparation. Nothing is live yet, and anyone who tells you otherwise is not speaking for us.

Is there a token?

No token has been announced. The design includes a native unit used for bonds and tithes, and the litepaper will describe it. The Foundation does not sell tokens, and we will never ask you to send funds to take part. Treat any offer to the contrary as a scam.

How is it different from other networks?

The aim is to be competitive with the most advanced networks on speed, cost and scale, with a design that is simpler to reason about and principles written down before the code. The field guide explains the design direction part by part.

Why are the components named after fungi?

Because the vocabulary carries a model of how the network works. Every term has a conventional equivalent in the glossary. There’s a longer answer in this field note.

When will the litepaper be published?

It is being written. We’ll announce it on the mailing list. You can see its planned outline on the litepaper page.

The Foundation

About the Foundation.

What does the Foundation do?

Open research, grants, stewardship of the specification, and education. See about.

Does the Foundation control the network?

No. It supports the network without owning or controlling it, and it intends to matter less over time.

How is the Foundation funded?

The Foundation is being formally established. Its funding sources, treasury policy and annual reports will be published as they are settled.

Are you related to other projects called Mycelium?

No. The Mycelium Foundation and the Mycelium Network are not affiliated with other projects or companies that use the name Mycelium.

Is Helen Atthowe involved?

No. We cite her work, and the work of other scientists and farmers, because it shaped ours. None of them is affiliated with the Foundation.

Taking part

Getting involved.

Can I run a node?

Not yet. The testnet is in preparation. Register your interest and we’ll reach out when it opens.

When does the first grant round open?

It’s being designed. Register interest and you’ll hear when it opens.

Do grants require equity or tokens?

No. Grants are not investments. We ask for delivery against agreed milestones and for funded work to be published openly.

Are you hiring?

Not yet. See careers for the roles we expect to open.

Safety

Staying safe.

Someone messaged me offering tokens or a presale.

It’s a scam. The Foundation does not sell tokens, run presales or airdrops, or contact people privately about investments.

How do I know a message is really from you?

We only write from addresses at mycelium.foundation, and we will never ask for private keys, seed phrases or payments.
