Privacy

Privacy notice.

Last updated 3 October 2026 · draft, pending legal review

This notice describes what this website does with information about you. It describes the site as it is built today; if that changes, so will this notice.

The short version

We don’t use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind. We don’t have accounts or forms. The site remembers two preferences in your own browser, and that’s all.

What your browser stores

The site uses your browser’s local storage for two things, which never leave your device:

  • myc-theme: whether you chose daylight or foxfire.
  • myc-tune: any settings you changed in the field tuner.

You can clear them at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Third parties

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses to operate and secure its service, under its own privacy statement.
  • Fonts. The JetBrains Mono typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser sends a request, including your IP address, to Google’s servers. Our display typeface is served from this site.

Email

If you write to us at hello@mycelium.foundation, we keep the correspondence so we can reply and follow up. We don’t add you to any list unless you ask us to, and we don’t share your address. Ask us and we’ll delete it.

Contact

Questions about this notice: hello@mycelium.foundation.
