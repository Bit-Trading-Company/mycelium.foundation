Code of conduct

Reciprocity, in practice.

Mycorrhizal partners reward those who trade fairly. Our spaces work the same way. This code applies to every space the Foundation runs, online and in person.

Be generous
Assume good faith, share what you know, and credit others’ work.
Be precise
Disagree with ideas, not people. Bring evidence; change your mind when it changes.
Be welcoming
Newcomers are the growing tips. Explain without condescension.
No harassment
No harassment, discrimination or intimidation of any kind, for any reason.
No promotion
No token shilling, price talk or unsolicited promotion. Scams are removed on sight.
Reporting
Write to hello@mycelium.foundation. Reports are handled confidentially, and we act on them.
