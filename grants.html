Grants

Feed the growing tips.

A mycelium sends nutrients to the edges where it’s growing. Our grants do the same: small, early support for the people building the network’s clients, tools, research and public goods.

The first grant round is being designed. Register interest below.

Translocation

How funding flows.

The tithe returns value to the commons. The commons funds the growing tips. Tips that deliver get more, and what they make returns to the commons as open work.

What we fund

Five kinds of work.

Clients

Independent implementations

More than one client keeps the network diverse above and below ground. A bug in one shouldn’t take down the rest.

Tooling

Tools for builders

Libraries, debuggers, indexers, wallets and testing tools that make the network pleasant to build on.

Research

Open research

Work on the open questions, from consensus proofs to fee dynamics, published for everyone.

Education

Teaching the network

Writing, courses, translations and diagrams that help more people understand how it works.

Public goods

Things everyone needs

Infrastructure no single team would build alone, but every team depends on.

Principles

How we decide.

Fund work, not winners
We support the work itself, and we’re glad when several teams take different approaches to the same problem.
Start small
Most grants begin as small seed grants. Larger support follows delivery, not promises.
Reinforce what delivers
Like a mycelium thickening the paths that find food, funding grows toward teams that ship.
Open by default
Grant-funded work is published under open licences, so it returns to the commons.
Diversity above and below ground
Many teams, many places, many approaches. A monoculture fails all at once.

Process

From spore to fruit.

The process we intend for the first round. It will be published in full when the round opens.

01Seedinquiry
A short note: what you want to build, why it matters for the network, and who you are.
02Conversationreview
A call with the grants team, and a written review by people with relevant expertise.
03Field trialseed grant
A small grant with a clear first milestone.
04Growthfollow-on grant
Larger support for teams that delivered, with milestones agreed in advance.
05Returnpublication
The work is published openly, and what was learned is written down for the next team.
