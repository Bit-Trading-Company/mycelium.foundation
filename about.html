About the Foundation

A nonprofit for a network that should outgrow it.

The Mycelium Foundation exists to help the Mycelium Network become healthy, neutral and widely held. Then, year by year, to matter less.

The Foundation is forming. Where this page describes structure or commitments, it describes what we intend; details will be published as they are settled.

Our role

We intend to be a nurse log.

In old forests, seedlings often grow from fallen trees. Fungi break the log down, and as they do it feeds what grows from it. In time it disappears into the forest it nourished.

A foundation that supports a decentralized network has an odd job. It has to do real work early, when the network is young and fragile, and it has to arrange things so that the network stops needing it. If we do this well, the most important parts of the network will be run, funded and improved by people who have never worked for us.

So we judge our decisions by a simple test, borrowed in spirit from the Ethereum Foundation’s: if the Foundation disappeared tomorrow, would the network keep growing? Every grant, hire and policy should move the answer closer to yes.

Four kinds of work.

Negative space

What we are not.

A foundation is defined as much by what it refuses to do as by what it does.

We are not

A company
We have no product to sell you and no customers to please.
The owner
The network belongs to the people who run it and build on it.
A kingmaker
We fund work, not winners, and we don’t pick favorites among teams.

Nor are we

A token promoter
We don’t sell tokens, predict prices or endorse anyone who does.
A gatekeeper
Anyone may build on, run or study the network without our permission.
Permanent
If the network thrives without us, we’ve done our job.

How we’re organized

Lean, and accountable.

The Foundation is being formally established. This is the shape we intend; names, governance documents and the legal entity will be published here as they are settled.

Boardstewardship
A small board responsible for the Foundation’s mission, finances and accountability, with members independent of any single team in the ecosystem.
Researchprotocol
A research group that works in the open, publishes its results, and collaborates with universities and independent researchers.
Grantsecosystem
Grant decisions reviewed by a panel that includes people from outside the Foundation, with conflicts of interest declared.
Operationslean
As few people as the work requires. We would rather fund ten outside teams than hire ten people.

Transparency

Accounts you can read.

A nonprofit that supports a public network should be as legible as the network itself. These are the commitments we intend to keep.

Annual reports
Each year: where our funding came from, what we spent it on, and every grant we made.
Treasury policy
How funds are held and managed, published before the testnet launches.
Conflicts of interest
Declared by board members, staff and grant reviewers, and published.
Open research
Research results published openly, under licences that let anyone build on them.
