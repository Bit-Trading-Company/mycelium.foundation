Careers

We’re not hiring yet.

The Foundation is forming, and we intend to stay small: we’d rather fund ten outside teams than hire ten people. When roles open, they’ll be listed here first.

Roles we expect

The first few people.

Protocol researcherresearch
Consensus, networking or mechanism design, with a habit of writing things down precisely.
Client engineerengineering
Systems programming, networking and an appetite for specifications.
Grants & ecosystemoperations
Someone who can read a proposal, talk to a team and judge whether the work matters.
Technical writereducation
Someone who can explain a consensus protocol to a curious stranger without dumbing it down.

How we hire

Plainly.

Work samples, not puzzles
We’ll ask to see work you’ve done, or pay you for a small piece of real work.
Remote and asynchronous
Written communication first, so people can work from anywhere.
Diversity above and below ground
Different places, backgrounds and disciplines make better networks and better teams.
