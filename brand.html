Brand kit

How the Foundation looks and sounds.

The mark, colors, type and voice, for anyone writing about or building around the Mycelium Network. The mark is a placeholder while the identity is finalized.

The mark

An M, drawn as a network.

Two legs and a V, joined at three nodes. The center node is where two hyphae met and fused: a quiet sign for agreement. It grows the same way on the homepage.

Clear space
Leave space equal to the width of one node around the mark.
Minimum size
16px for the favicon version, 24px for the full mark.
Don’t
Don’t recolor it outside the palette, add effects, rotate it, or draw a mushroom near it.

Color

Paper, ink and green.

Daylight is an off-white paper with forest-black ink. Foxfire, the dark theme, is soil with a bioluminescent glow. Each section of the site also retints the field.

  • Paper#F3F4EEground, daylight
  • Ink#0D1610text
  • Fern#1B5528links, labels
  • Glow#5C9A12signals, tips
  • Soil#0B110Dground, foxfire
  • Foxfire#C3F27Aglow, foxfire

Type

Styrene and JetBrains Mono.

Display · Styrene A

Grown, not built.

ThinLightRegularMedium

Headlines in Light; headings in Regular; small emphasis in Medium; Thin only for the wordmark. Neutral tracking.

Text & interface · JetBrains Mono

Each hyphal tip follows a few local rules, and the colony’s intelligence comes from everyone following them at once.

Voice

Plain, patient, precise.

Plain
Short sentences, common words. Explain, don’t sell.
Humble about us
The network matters; the Foundation is one participant.
Honest about science
Say what’s known, what’s debated, and what’s a metaphor.
No hype
No price talk, no superlatives, no claims without data.
Names
“The Mycelium Network”, “the network”, “The Mycelium Foundation”, “the Foundation”. Mycelial terms are lowercase except Quorum Pulse.
